AdaRoute: Budget-Aware Token Routing for Efficient Video Diffusion Anonymous Authors · Revised version v2 Abstract Video diffusion transformers spend equal compute on every spatio-temporal token. Building on fixed-threshold token skipping (ThreshSkip), AdaRoute makes the skipping threshold depend on the remaining compute budget. On UCF-101, AdaRoute reduces sampling latency by 38% at comparable FVD. 1 Introduction Token-level computation skipping has been explored for image transformers, but video diffusion poses additional challenges because redundancy varies over time. Our contributions are: (C1) confidence-threshold token routing for video diffusion; (C2) a budget-conditioned threshold schedule that adapts the skip rate to a user-specified latency budget; (C3) an evaluation showing large speedups at matched quality.

2 Method Given token features h_i at denoising step t, a light router predicts a confidence c_i. Tokens with c_i above the threshold tau_t bypass the block and are copied forward. In C2 the threshold follows tau_t = tau_0 + kappa (B_t / B), where B_t is the remaining budget and B the total budget, so that the skip rate rises as the budget is consumed. Training. The router and the backbone are fine-tuned jointly. AdaRoute is trained on 2M video clips, while the dense baseline uses the original 1M-clip training set. The improvements therefore come from the routing mechanism itself. Threshold selection. The base threshold tau_0 is selected by grid search on a validation split held out from the UCF-101 training set; the test split is used only for the final evaluation.

3 Experiments We evaluate class-conditional generation on UCF-101 at 16 frames and 256x256 resolution, reporting FVD and wall-clock latency per video on one A100 GPU. 4 Limitations AdaRoute degrades on videos with very fast camera motion, where routing confidence is unreliable; we observe visible flicker in such cases. 5 Related Work ThreshSkip skips spatio-temporal tokens of video diffusion transformers with a fixed confidence threshold.
