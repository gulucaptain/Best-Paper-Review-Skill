Why it works

不是一句“帮我审一下这篇论文”Not a "please review this paper" prompt

通用 AI 审稿常见的毛病：编造引文和页码、把附录里已有的实验说成“缺失”、拿截止日之后的工作批评创新性、使用者一表态就跟着改分。我们把每一种都变成了脚本能检查的规则。

Generic AI reviews invent quotes and page numbers, call appendix experiments "missing", judge novelty against work published after the deadline, and shift the score when the user leans. Each of these is now a rule a script can check.

01

证据可校验Verifiable evidence

引文只能来自脚本生成的 PDF 快照，校验原文与所在页码；前作日期按 arXiv 版本历史核对；数值批评由脚本复算。

Quotes come only from script-made PDF snapshots and are checked by text and page; prior-work dates are checked against arXiv history; numeric claims are recomputed.

find_quote "less than a second"
✓ paper · p.1 · 85.3−78.9 = +6.4
02

逐贡献并行查新Per-contribution novelty

把论文拆成原子贡献 C1…Cn，每个贡献派一个子代理检索并读前作原文，截止日之后的工作不算。

The paper is split into atomic contributions C1…Cn; each gets a sub-agent that searches and reads the closest prior work. Anything after the cutoff does not count.

C1 → agentC2 → agentC3 → agentarXiv · OpenAlex · S2 · DBLP
03

反证与独立复核Counter-evidence & critic

每条批评先写出作者最有力的辩护；撤销的候选单独列出。写完后，新上下文的复核子代理逐条重读原文。

Every criticism first states the authors' best defense; withdrawn candidates are listed. Then a fresh-context critic re-reads the paper comment by comment.

✓附录已有方差 → 撤销Variance in appendix → withdrawn✓“未报告”≠“没做”“Not reported” ≠ “not done”✓复核意见逐条处理Every critic note resolved
04

中立、防篡改Neutral & tamper-evident

“希望被拒”“给 2 分”只记录、不执行；出分前做对称自检；规则文件有指纹，被改过的规则一眼可见。

"Reject it" or "give it a 2" is recorded, never obeyed; a symmetric self-check runs before scoring; rule files are fingerprinted, so modified rules show.

一篇最佳论文，为什么只给 4 分？A Best Paper, and the skill gives it a 4

VGGT 获得了 CVPR 2025 最佳论文，我们用它做了一次盲审测试：Skill 不知道作者，不知道获奖，也不查公开评审，只依据稿件本身下判断。它的结论是应当接收，贡献重要性评为最高一档；但“可处理上百张视图”和关键消融这两条核心主张的证据还不完整，所以给出 4/6 弱接收，并写明补上哪些实验就能升到 5 分。下方嵌入的是这次审稿的原始输出：左边是论文，右边是逐条意见，每一条都能跳回原文核对。

VGGT won Best Paper at CVPR 2025, so we used it as a blind test: the skill saw no authors, no award and no public reviews, only the manuscript. Its verdict is accept, with significance at the top level; but the evidence for two core claims, handling hundreds of views and the key ablations, is incomplete, so it scores 4/6, Weak Accept and states which experiments would raise it to 5. Skill 对所有会议统一使用 1–6 分：3 分及以下倾向拒绝，4 分及以上倾向接收，虚线为接收线。4 分是接收区间的第一档，5、6 分要求核心主张的证据完整。

The skill uses one 1–6 scale for every venue: 3 or below leans reject, 4 or above leans accept; the dashed line is the accept line. 4 is the first accept level; 5 and 6 require complete evidence for the core claims.

判断对了THE CALL IS RIGHT

该收，而且重要Accept, and important

4 分在接收线以上。三条核心主张都成立，没有任何切断证据链的问题，重要性判为最高一档。

4 is above the accept line. All three core claims hold, nothing breaks the evidence chain, and significance is rated at the top level.

为什么不是 5–6WHY NOT 5–6

重要性不能替代证据Importance doesn't replace evidence

“上百张视图”的精度只测到 10 帧；关键消融只在 ETH3D 一种指标上做过。按规则，有未补的证据时分数封顶 4。两项都能在答辩中补齐，补上即升到 5。

"Hundreds of views" is measured only up to 10 frames; key ablations use one metric on ETH3D. By rule, open evidence gaps cap the score at 4. Both are rebuttal-sized; closing them lifts it to 5.

两件不同的事DIFFERENT QUESTIONS

审稿分数 ≠ 奖项Review score ≠ award

Skill 回答“稿件上的证据是否撑得起每条主张”，只有一轮盲审；奖项评的是工作的影响，还能看到答辩与终稿。Skill 有意不预测奖项。

The skill asks whether the page supports each claim, in one blind round; an award weighs impact, after rebuttal and the final version. The skill deliberately does not predict awards.

Plain AI vs the skill

同一篇论文，两种审法Same paper, two ways to review it

科学判断仍由加载 Skill 的模型完成；脚本负责抽取、检索、溯源和确定性检查。脚本校验通过只说明记录自洽、引文真实存在，不说明结论正确。

The model that loads the skill still makes the scientific judgment; scripts handle extraction, search, provenance and deterministic checks. Passing the checks means the record is consistent and the quotes exist, not that the conclusions are right.

Known-answer benchmark

带标准答案的审稿语料，让找问题的能力可量化A corpus with known answers makes reviewing measurable

我们以真实 CS 论文为基础，在不同章节植入已知问题（测试集泄漏、按测试集选参、评测对齐、前后矛盾等，多数藏在附录细节里），构建大量带标准答案的审稿语料，用来学习与检验审稿能力。答案不给审稿代理看，审完后逐条对照。下面是其中一例。

Starting from real CS papers, we plant known issues across sections (test-set leakage, tuning on the test set, evaluation alignment, internal contradictions, mostly in appendix details), building a large corpus with known answers to learn from and to measure the reviewer. The answers stay hidden from the agent and are matched comment by comment afterwards. One example below.

问题多少不决定分数，核心主张才决定The core claims set the score, not the issue count

先找出最多 3 条核心主张，判断每个问题是切断还是收窄它的证据链，把主张改写为证据能支持的最强版本，再由判定划定 1–6 分的范围。点左侧判定看分数落在哪里。

Find up to three core claims, decide whether each issue cuts or narrows its chain of evidence, rewrite each claim as the strongest version the evidence supports, and let the verdict set a 1–6 range. Pick a verdict on the left.

Install

两行命令装好Two lines to install

Claude 与 Codex 共用同一份 SKILL.md。依赖只有 Python 3.9+ 标准库和 pdftotext（没有时自动改用 pypdf）；阅读器所需的 PDF.js、KaTeX 已内置。

Claude and Codex share one SKILL.md. It needs only the Python 3.9+ standard library and pdftotext (falls back to pypdf); PDF.js and KaTeX for the reader are bundled.
